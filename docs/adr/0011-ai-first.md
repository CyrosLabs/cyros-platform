# ADR-0011 — AI-First Development

Status: Accepted

## Context

Artificial Intelligence is a core capability across nearly every Cyros product.

Engineering workflows can also benefit from AI-assisted development.

## Decision

Adopt an AI-first engineering approach.

AI is considered:

- A platform capability.
- A development accelerator.
- A productivity multiplier.

AI assists engineering but does not replace engineering judgment.

## Consequences

Positive

- Faster development.
- Better documentation.
- Higher productivity.

Negative

- Requires prompt engineering.
- Requires human validation.

## Alternatives Considered

- AI only within products.
- No AI-assisted development.
