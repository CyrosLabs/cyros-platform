# Engineering Principles

## Human First

Every decision starts with the user.

---

## Simplicity

Complexity is a cost.

Prefer simple solutions whenever possible.

---

## Build for the Long Term

Write software we can still be proud of five years from now.

---

## Learn Continuously

Technology evolves.

So do we.

---

## Quality Over Quantity

Build fewer features.

Build them exceptionally well.

---

## AI as Amplification

Artificial Intelligence should amplify human potential—not replace it.

---

## Delight

Every interaction should feel thoughtful.
