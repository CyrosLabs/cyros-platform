# Company Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

Establish the engineering platform and development standards.

- Company vision
- Engineering culture
- Shared platform
- Design system
- Developer platform
- First product architecture

---

## Phase 2 — First Product

Launch Haru Haru.

Goals:

- MVP
- TOPIK I
- User authentication
- Progress tracking
- Gamification
- Subscription system

---

## Phase 3 — Connected Products

Expand into additional life pillars.

Potential products include:

- Companion
- Goals
- Challenges
- Diet Buddy
- Workout
- Diet Planner

---

## Phase 4 — Shared Platform

Extract common services into reusable platform capabilities.

- Identity
- AI Platform
- Payments
- Notifications
- Analytics
- Recommendation Engine

---

## Phase 5 — Ecosystem

Create an interconnected ecosystem where products work together.

Examples:

- Goals integrates with Companion.
- Workout integrates with Diet Buddy.
- Pantry integrates with Diet Planner.
- Haru Haru celebrates learning milestones through Companion.

---

## Phase 6 — Life OS

Unify every Cyros product into a connected ecosystem that helps people thrive.

Life OS is not a single application.

It is the experience created when every Cyros product works together seamlessly.
