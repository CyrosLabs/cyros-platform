# Haru Haru Vision

## Overview

Haru Haru is a Korean learning companion designed to help people build confidence and consistency while learning Korean.

Traditional language apps often focus on memorization and short-term engagement.

Haru Haru focuses on meaningful progress.

The goal is not only to teach vocabulary and grammar.

The goal is to help learners become confident Korean speakers step by step.

---

## Vision Statement

To create a learning companion that helps people learn Korean through meaningful, progressive, and motivating experiences.

---

## Mission

Haru Haru makes Korean learning accessible by transforming language acquisition into a personalized journey where every small achievement builds confidence.

---

## Philosophy

Learning a language is not only about knowing words.

It is about opening the door to a new culture, new connections, and a new version of yourself.

Every lesson should leave the learner feeling:

"I am improving."

---

## Target Users

### Beginner Korean Learners

People who:

- Want to start learning Korean.
- Enjoy Korean culture.
- Feel overwhelmed by traditional learning methods.
- Need structure and motivation.

---

## Core Principles

### Progress Over Perfection

Small consistent progress creates long-term success.

---

### Learning Should Feel Rewarding

The experience should feel closer to a game than a textbook.

---

### Confidence Matters

Users should feel capable after every lesson.

---

### Personalized Growth

The system should adapt to each learner's journey.

---

## Long Term Vision

Haru Haru evolves from a Korean learning app into an AI-powered language companion that helps users:

- Learn Korean.
- Practice conversation.
- Improve pronunciation.
- Understand culture.
- Build confidence.
